    Data notes. All share prices, volumes, ranges and price history load live from Yahoo Finance (BAMNB.AS, HEIJM.AS, ^AEX), fetched by the included server.py on the same device (or, as a fallback, a public CORS proxy), which passes the data on unchanged. Exchange data may be delayed by about 15 minutes. If Yahoo cannot be reached, prices show as unavailable; no stored or modelled prices are used. Market caps are estimates: live price × shares in issue (BAM about 253m, Heijmans about 27.5m). Headlines load live from the Google News RSS feed and are refreshed every 10 minutes; if the feed cannot be reached, no news is shown. Revenue, order book and margin figures come from the H1 2026 reports. Returns exclude dividends. On GitHub Pages, a scheduled job fetches the same data every 15 minutes during Euronext hours and the page shows when it was last updated. Live data only loads when this file is opened directly in a browser, through server.py, or on GitHub Pages. Not investment advice.
